=== IEEE BrokenShells - Challenge 3: OSINT ===

A student at my department arrived in Volos with her electric car. Her battery 
has max capacitance of 50kWh and she had 20%. She stopped at the charger of the
photo and sent a message to the groupchat "I'm charging till 80% and I'm coming".

Question 1: What's the general area where the charger is at called?
Question 2: Who is the operator/company of the charger and what the charger max power (in kW)?
Question 3: How many minutes will they need to charge from 20% to 80%? (Assume that the charger
is charging at max of his power constantly with no losses.)

Flag Format: BSHELL{AREANAME_COMPANY_MINUTES}
