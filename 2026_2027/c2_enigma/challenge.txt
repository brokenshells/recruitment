=== IEEE BrokenShells - Challenge 2: Enigma ===

We intercepted one Enigma I (M3) message. We also recovered the full
machine settings, including the message key. Use the provided
EnigmaMachine class to decrypt it, then read the flag out of the plaintext.

SETTINGS
  Rotors (left to right): I II III
  Ring settings:          1 1 1
  Reflector:              B
  Plugboard:              AM FI NV PS TU WZ
  Message key (start):    VOL

CIPHERTEXT
GFBQPBCXSIEMHNJNDMCPVCHQUMHZHDAXTTYJMQVFMPIDGYSXFLTDTCBKKJCFUMNVQMRVXGAJJZLMC

The flag is BSHELL{...}. Enigma only enciphers A-Z, so the plaintext
spells the flag body in letters: find FLAGIS...X and wrap it in BSHELL{}.
